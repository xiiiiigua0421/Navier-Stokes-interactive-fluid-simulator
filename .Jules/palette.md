## 2024-05-24 - Dynamic Brush Cursor Feedback
**Learning:** Proactive visual feedback for dynamic, time-based states (like a color-changing brush) significantly enhances user confidence by showing exactly what will happen before an interaction occurs.
**Action:** When a tool has a time-varying property (like color or size), always display its current state in the cursor or hover preview so the user isn't guessing what the outcome of their click will be.
