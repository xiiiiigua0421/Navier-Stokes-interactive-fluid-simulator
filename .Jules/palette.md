## 2025-02-27 - [Add Visual Brush Boundaries for Painting Interactions]
**Learning:** [In Pygame/canvas fluid simulations where users draw or inject elements, the default arrow cursor provides no indication of the area of effect (brush size). Adding a dynamic cursor change (e.g., crosshair) combined with a visual outline of the brush radius dramatically improves interaction confidence, making the "painting" affordance clear. Also, changing `pygame.mouse.set_cursor` in headless/dummy video driver environments can throw errors and must be wrapped in `try/except pygame.error`.]
**Action:** [When implementing paint or injection interactions on a canvas, always provide a visual indicator of the brush/brush radius. Revert to standard cursors outside the interaction zone, and safeguard cursor API calls against headless test environments.]
